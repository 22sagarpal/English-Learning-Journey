2. Grammar: Countable & Uncountable Nouns

Countable Nouns

Jinhe gina ja sakta hai.

Examples:

One book, two books

One apple, three apples

One pen, five pens


Use:

Many

A few


Examples:

I have many books.

She has a few apples.


Uncountable Nouns

Jinhe seedha gina nahi ja sakta.

Examples:

Water

Milk

Rice

Sugar

Money


Use:

Much

A little


Examples:

I drink much water. (A2 level par samajhne ke liye)

I have a little money.

2. What do you usually buy?


3. Do you prefer cash or online payment?


4. What is the most expensive thing you own?


5. Do you look for discounts while shopping?

4. Reading Practice

Read:

> Yesterday, Riya went shopping with her mother. She bought a blue dress and a pair of shoes. The dress was on discount, so it was cheaper than usual. After paying, she received a receipt and returned home happily.



Questions

1. Where did Riya go?


2. Who went with her?


3. What did she buy?


4. Why was the dress cheaper?


5. What did she receive after paying?

5. Writing Practice

7. Shopping Conversation

Customer: How much does this shirt cost?

Shopkeeper: It costs 500 rupees.

Customer: Do you have a discount?

Shopkeeper: Yes, there is a 10% discount.

Customer: Great! I will buy it.
