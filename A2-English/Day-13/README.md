2. Grammar — Before & After

Before = Pehle

I brush my teeth before breakfast.

I study before dinner.

Wash your hands before eating.


After = Baad mein

I go to college after breakfast.

I watch TV after dinner.

I take a rest after studying.


Easy Formula 🧠

Before + Activity 1 → Activity 2

> I study before dinner.



After + Activity 1 → Activity 2

> I sleep after dinner.
