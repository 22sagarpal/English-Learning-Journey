2. Grammar: Past Simple Tense

Structure

Subject + Verb (2nd Form) + Object

Examples

I visited Delhi last year.

She watched a movie yesterday.

We played cricket on Sunday.

They traveled by train.


Negative

Subject + did not + Verb (1st Form)

I did not visit Delhi.

She did not watch TV.


Question

Did + Subject + Verb (1st Form)?

Did you visit Agra?

Did she watch the movie?
