2. Grammar — Object Pronouns

Object pronoun ka use tab hota hai jab koi person action ko receive karta hai.

Subject	Object

I	me
You	you
He	him
She	her
It	it
We	us
They	them


Examples

She called me.

I helped him.

He invited her.

Please send it to me.

They called us.

We visited them.


Compare:

I called him.
➡️ I = action kar raha hai
➡️ him = action receive kar raha hai

5. Reading Practice 📖

Read this:

> Ravi received a message from his friend yesterday. His friend invited him to a birthday party. Ravi replied to him and said that he would come. Later, Ravi called his sister and told her about the party. She also wanted to join them.



Questions

1. Who sent Ravi a message?


2. What did his friend invite him to?


3. What did Ravi do after receiving the message?


4. Who did Ravi call?


5. Did his sister want to join them?
