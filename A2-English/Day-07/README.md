2. Grammar: Adverbs of Frequency

Ye batate hain ki koi kaam kitni baar hota hai.

Adverb	Meaning

Always	Hamesha
Usually	Aam taur par
Often	Aksar
Sometimes	Kabhi-kabhi
Rarely	Bahut kam
Never	Kabhi nahi


Structure

Subject + Adverb + Verb

Examples:

I always wake up early.

She usually reads books.

We often play cricket.

They sometimes watch movies.

He never smokes.

4. Reading Practice

Read:

> Amit loves reading books. He usually reads for one hour every evening. Sometimes, he visits the library to borrow new books. Reading helps him learn new things and improve his vocabulary. It is his favorite hobby.



Questions

1. What is Amit's hobby?


2. How long does he read every evening?


3. Where does he sometimes go?


4. How does reading help him?


5. Is reading his favorite hobby?

5. Writing Practice
