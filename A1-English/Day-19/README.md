2. Grammar – Adjectives

Adjective किसी noun की quality बताता है।

Examples:

a big house

a small bag

a beautiful girl

a new phone

an old car


Sentence में:

The house is big.

The bag is small.

The phone is new.

The car is old.

The room is clean.



---

3. Adjective + Noun

Adjective आमतौर पर noun से पहले आता है।

Adjective + Noun

big house

small room

new phone

old book

beautiful flower


❌ house big
✅ big house

लेकिन be (am/is/are) के बाद adjective भी आ सकता है:

The house is big.

The flower is beautiful.

The room is clean.

5. Describing People 🧑
