2. Grammar – Prepositions of Place

Prepositions बताते हैं कि कोई व्यक्ति या वस्तु कहाँ है।

In (में)

The book is in the bag.

I am in the room.


On (पर)

The phone is on the table.

The picture is on the wall.


Under (के नीचे)

The cat is under the chair.


Behind (के पीछे)

The car is behind the house.


In front of (के सामने)

The school is in front of the park.


Near (के पास)

My house is near the market.


Between (के बीच)

The bank is between the school and the hospital.
