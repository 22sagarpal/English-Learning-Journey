2. Grammar – Have / Has

Have / Has का इस्तेमाल किसी चीज़ के पास होने के लिए करते हैं।

Have

I, You, We, They + have

I have a phone.

You have a book.

We have a car.

They have money.


Has

He, She, It + has

He has a laptop.

She has a bag.

He has a bicycle.

She has a new phone.



---

3. Negative Sentences

Don't have

I, You, We, They के साथ:

I don't have a car.

We don't have money.

They don't have a laptop.


Doesn't have

He, She, It के साथ:

He doesn't have a car.

She doesn't have a laptop.


⚠️ ध्यान दो:

❌ He doesn't has a car.
✅ He doesn't have a car.

4. Questions

Do + subject + have...?

Do you have a phone?

Do they have a car?

Do we have a class today?


Does + subject + have...?

Does he have a laptop?

Does she have a bicycle?


Answers

Yes, I do.
No, I don't.

Yes, he does.
No, he doesn't.
