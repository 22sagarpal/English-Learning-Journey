2. Grammar – Future with “Will”

भविष्य में होने वाले काम के लिए:

Formula

Subject + will + V1

I will study.

You will learn.

He will come.

She will help.

We will travel.

They will work.


⚠️ Will के बाद हमेशा Verb का first form (V1) आता है।

❌ I will went.
✅ I will go.

❌ She will studied.
✅ She will study.


---

3. Negative Sentences

will not = won't

I will not go.

I won't go.

He won't come.

She won't call.

We won't travel tomorrow.



---

4. Questions

Will + Subject + V1?

Will you come tomorrow?

Will you study tonight?

Will he help me?

Will she call you?

Will they visit us?


Short Answers

Yes, I will.

No, I won't.
