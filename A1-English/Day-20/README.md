2. Grammar – Comparative Adjectives

जब हम दो लोगों या चीज़ों की तुलना करते हैं, तो अक्सर adjective के साथ -er लगाते हैं।

Short Adjectives

adjective + er + than

tall → taller

small → smaller

fast → faster

slow → slower

cheap → cheaper


Examples

Ram is taller than Amit.

A car is faster than a bicycle.

This bag is cheaper than that bag.

My room is smaller than your room.



---

3. Important Spelling Rules

Big → Bigger

अंतिम consonant double होता है:

big → bigger

Hot → Hotter

hot → hotter

Easy → Easier

y → i + er

easy → easier

Large → Larger

अगर word के अंत में e है:

large → larger

4. Irregular Comparatives

कुछ words अलग तरह से बदलते हैं:

Adjective	Comparative

Good	Better
Bad	Worse
Far	Farther/Further


Examples:

This book is better than that book.

Today is better than yesterday.

This road is worse than that road.



---

5. Using “Than”

Than = से

Sagar is taller than Rahul.

A car is faster than a bicycle.

Tea is cheaper than coffee.

My phone is better than my old phone.
