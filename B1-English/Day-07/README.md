2. Grammar — Modal Verbs

Modal verbs का इस्तेमाल advice, obligation, permission, ability और possibility बताने के लिए किया जाता है।

Should → सलाह

> You should practice English every day.



> You should get enough sleep.



Must → बहुत जरूरी / strong obligation

> You must follow the rules.



> You must submit your assignment today.



Have to → मजबूरी / आवश्यकता

> I have to attend college tomorrow.



> She has to complete her project.



Could → Ability / Possibility / Polite Request

> I could swim when I was young.



> It could rain today.



> Could you help me?



Might → संभावना

> It might rain tonight.



> He might come tomorrow.




---

3. Should vs Must vs Have to

याद रखने का आसान तरीका:

Should = करना चाहिए
Must = जरूर करना है
Have to = करना पड़ता है

Examples:

You should learn English.

You must carry your ID card.

I have to go to college.
