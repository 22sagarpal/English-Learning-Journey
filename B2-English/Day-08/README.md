2. Grammar – Past Perfect

Past Perfect ka use tab hota hai jab past mein do actions hue hon aur batana ho ki pehle kaunsa hua.

Structure

Subject + had + V3

> I had finished my work before he arrived.



Pehle → finished
Baad mein → arrived

Examples

> She had left before I reached the station.



> They had completed the project before the deadline.



> I had never seen such a beautiful place before.




---

3. Past Perfect Continuous

Jab past mein koi activity kuch time se continue ho rahi thi, tab iska use karte hain.

Structure

Subject + had been + Verb-ing + for/since

> I had been studying for three hours when my friend called.



> She had been working there since 2020 before she changed jobs.



Difference

Past Perfect:

> I had studied English before I moved to Delhi.



Focus → completed action

Past Perfect Continuous:

> I had been studying English for two years before I moved to Delhi.



Focus → duration/continuity

5. Reading Practice 📖

Read carefully:

> Before starting his first job, Rahul had completed his degree and had developed several technical skills. He had also been preparing for interviews for several months. Although he had faced many difficulties during his preparation, he remained consistent. By the time he attended his first interview, he had become much more confident and was able to answer most questions clearly.



Questions

1. What had Rahul completed before starting his job?


2. How long had he been preparing for interviews?


3. Why had he become more confident?


4. What was he able to do during the interview?

8. Daily Conversation

A: What had you learned before you started working on your current skills?

B: Before I started focusing on advanced communication, I had already learned basic English grammar and vocabulary.

A: Had you been practicing regularly?

B: Yes, I had been practicing for some time, although I wasn't very confident about speaking.

A: What changed eventually?

B: Regular practice helped me become more comfortable with English. I realized that making mistakes is a normal part of learning.
