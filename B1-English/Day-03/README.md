2. Grammar: Comparative & Superlative Adjectives

Comparative (2 things compare)

Adjective + er + than

Examples:

Ram is taller than Shyam.

English is easier than Japanese.

This book is better than that one.


Superlative (3 or more)

the + adjective + est

Examples:

Mount Everest is the highest mountain.

He is the smartest student in the class.

This is the best movie I have seen.
