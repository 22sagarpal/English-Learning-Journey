2. Grammar — Participle Clauses

C1 English mein sentences ko concise aur sophisticated banane ke liye participle clauses useful hain.

Normal:

Because he was tired, he went home.

Advanced:

Being tired, he went home.

Normal:

After she finished her work, she went to bed.

Advanced:

Having finished her work, she went to bed.

More Examples

Having completed the project, we submitted the report.

Knowing the risks, he decided to continue.

Faced with a difficult decision, she remained calm.


⚠️ Subject logically same hona chahiye.

3. Advanced Expressions

Instead of saying:

I think...

Use:

From my perspective...

In my view...

I would argue that...

It could be argued that...

There is a strong case for...

I am inclined to believe that...


Instead of:

It is very important.

Use:

It is of considerable importance.

It plays a crucial role.

It is essential to...

It is fundamental to...

5. Reading Practice 📖

Read carefully:

> Part-time employment can provide students with valuable practical experience. By working alongside their studies, students may develop communication, teamwork, and time-management skills. However, excessive working hours can diminish academic performance and increase stress. Therefore, the ideal approach is to maintain a reasonable balance between employment and education.



Questions

1. What benefits can part-time employment provide?


2. What can excessive working hours cause?


3. What balance does the passage recommend?


4. Find a word meaning "reduce".


5. Find a word meaning "working together".
