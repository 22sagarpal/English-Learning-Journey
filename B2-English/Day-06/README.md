2. Grammar – Relative Clauses

Relative Clauses ka use kisi person, place ya thing ke baare mein extra information dene ke liye hota hai.

Who → People

> The man who lives next door is a doctor.



Which → Things

> This is the laptop which I bought yesterday.



That → People / Things

> The book that I purchased is interesting.



Where → Places

> This is the college where I study.



Whose → Possession

> I met a student whose project won the competition.




---

Compare 👇

Simple:

> I know a student. He speaks Japanese.



Better B2 sentence:

> I know a student who speaks Japanese.



Simple:

> This is the company. I want to work there.



Better:

> This is the company where I want to work.

3. Advanced Sentence Building 🧠

Simple sentence:

> I am learning Python.



B2-style sentence:

> I am learning Python because I believe it will help me build a successful career in technology.



Another:

> Although Python can be challenging at first, regular practice can make it much easier to understand.



Useful Connectors

Although — Halanki

However — Lekin

Therefore — Isliye

Moreover — Iske alawa

In addition — Atirikt roop se

As a result — Parinaamswaroop

On the other hand — Doosri taraf

For example — Udaharan ke liye

4. Reading Practice 📖

Read carefully:

> Learning a language requires patience and consistency. Although memorizing vocabulary is useful, simply knowing individual words is not enough to communicate effectively. Learners should also understand how words are used in real situations. Moreover, regular speaking and listening practice can gradually improve fluency and confidence. As a result, students who practice consistently are more likely to communicate naturally over time.



Questions

1. Why is memorizing vocabulary alone not enough?


2. What can improve fluency and confidence?


3. What is the main message of the paragraph?

Basic:

> "I am hardworking."

> "I consider myself a hardworking person because I try to remain consistent even when something becomes difficult."

7. Daily Conversation

A: What kind of career do you want in the future?

B: I want to build a career in the technology industry, particularly in the field of data analytics.

A: Why did you choose this field?

B: I chose it because I enjoy working with data and solving problems. Moreover, I believe that data-related skills will remain valuable as technology continues to develop.

A: What are you doing to achieve this goal?

B: I am improving my technical skills while also working on my English communication because both are important for professional growth.
