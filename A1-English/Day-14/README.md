2. Grammar – Was / Were

Was / Were = था / थी / थे

Was

I, He, She, It + was

I was tired.

He was happy.

She was at home.

It was cold.


Were

You, We, They + were

You were busy.

We were at college.

They were happy.


Easy Trick 🧠

I → was

He / She / It → was

You / We / They → were


---

3. Present → Past

Present	Past

I am happy.	I was happy.
He is busy.	He was busy.
She is at home.	She was at home.
We are tired.	We were tired.
They are at college.	They were at college.



---

4. Negative Sentences

Was not → wasn't

I wasn't tired.

He wasn't at home.

She wasn't busy.


Were not → weren't

We weren't late.

They weren't happy.

You weren't busy.



---

5. Questions

Was...?

Was he at home?

Was she tired?

Was it cold?


Yes, he was.
No, he wasn't.

Were...?

Were you busy?

Were they at college?

Were we late?


Yes, we were.
No, we weren't.
