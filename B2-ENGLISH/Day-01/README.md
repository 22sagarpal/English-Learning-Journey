2. Grammar – Present Perfect Tense

Structure

Subject + has/have + V3 + Object

Examples

I have completed my work.

She has finished her homework.

We have learned Python.

They have visited Delhi.


Negative

I have not completed my work.

She has not finished her homework.


Questions

Have you completed your work?

Has she finished her homework?


Exercise

Translate into English:

1. Maine apna kaam poora kar liya hai.


2. Humne Python seekh li hai.


3. Kya tumne khana kha liya hai?


4. Usne apna project complete kar liya hai.


5. Maine abhi tak movie nahi dekhi hai.
