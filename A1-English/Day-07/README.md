2. Grammar – WH Questions

What (क्या)

What is your name?

What do you study?


Where (कहाँ)

Where do you live?

Where is your college?


When (कब)

When do you wake up?

When is your exam?


Who (कौन)

Who is your friend?

Who is your teacher?


Why (क्यों)

Why are you happy?

Why do you study English?


Answer: Because I want to improve my English.


---

3. Examples

What is your name?

My name is Sagar.


Where do you live?

I live in India.


When do you study?

I study in the evening.


Who is your best friend?

Rohit is my best friend.


Why do you learn English?

Because I want a good career.
