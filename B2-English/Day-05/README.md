2. Grammar – Reported Speech 🗣️

Reported Speech ka use kisi ke words ko directly quote kiye bina batane ke liye hota hai.

Direct Speech

> Rahul said, "I am busy."



Reported Speech

> Rahul said that he was busy.



Common Changes

Present → Past

am/is → was

are → were

have/has → had

do/does → did

will → would

can → could


Examples

Direct: She said, "I am tired."
Reported: She said that she was tired.

Direct: He said, "I will call you."
Reported: He said that he would call me.

Direct: They said, "We can help you."
Reported: They said that they could help me.

Questions

Direct: He asked, "Are you ready?"
Reported: He asked me if I was ready.

6. Daily Conversation

A: What skills do you think are important for getting a good job?

B: I think technical skills, communication, problem-solving, and teamwork are essential.

A: Which skill are you currently working on?

B: I am currently improving my English communication skills. I have been practicing speaking, vocabulary, grammar, and writing regularly.

A: How will this help you?

B: It will help me communicate more confidently during interviews and in a professional environment.
