2. Grammar – Do / Does Questions

Use "Do"

I, You, We, They के साथ

Do you study English?

Do they play cricket?

Do we have class today?


Use "Does"

He, She, It के साथ

Does he play cricket?

Does she study English?

Does it work?


Short Answers

Yes, I do.
No, I don't.

Yes, he does.
No, he doesn't.


---

3. Examples

Do you speak English? → Yes, I do.

Do you read books? → Yes, I do.

Does he play football? → No, he doesn't.

Does she study in college? → Yes, she does.
