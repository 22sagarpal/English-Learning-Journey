3. Grammar: Inversion

Normal:

I have never seen such a beautiful place.

Inversion:

Never have I seen such a beautiful place.

More Examples:

Rarely do we get such opportunities.

Not only did he pass, but he also topped the class.

Hardly had I arrived when it started raining.
