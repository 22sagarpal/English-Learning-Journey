2. Grammar – Irregular Verbs

Go → Went

I go to college every day.

I went to college yesterday.


Eat → Ate

I eat breakfast every morning.

I ate breakfast yesterday.


See → Saw

I see my friend every day.

I saw my friend yesterday.


Come → Came

He comes home at 6 PM.

He came home yesterday.



---

3. Important Rule ⚠️

Did के बाद हमेशा V1 आता है।

❌ Did you went to college?
✅ Did you go to college?

❌ I didn't ate breakfast.
✅ I didn't eat breakfast.

❌ Did she saw him?
✅ Did she see him?

यही rule याद रखो:

Did + Subject + V1
