2. Grammar: Superlative Adjectives

Superlative ka use 3 ya usse zyada cheezon me sabse best ya sabse zyada batane ke liye hota hai.

Structure

Subject + be + the + adjective-est

Examples

Mount Everest is the highest mountain.

Rahul is the tallest boy in the class.

This is the cheapest shop in the market.


Irregular Forms

Positive	Superlative

Good	The best
Bad	The worst
Far	The farthest


Examples

This is the best book I have read.

It was the worst day of my life.
