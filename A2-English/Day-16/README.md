2. Grammar — Have / Has

Have / Has ka use possession (kisi cheez ka hona) aur kuch common conditions ke liye hota hai.

I / You / We / They → Have

I have a headache.

You have a fever.

We have a problem.

They have a cold.


He / She / It → Has

He has a headache.

She has a cough.

My brother has a fever.


Negative

I don't have a fever.

She doesn't have a headache.


Questions

Do you have a headache?

Does he have a fever?


⚠️ Important:

❌ He have a fever.
✅ He has a fever.

❌ Does he has a fever?
✅ Does he have a fever?

4. Reading Practice 📖

Read this:

> Rahul does not feel well today. He has a headache and a cough. He decides to rest at home. His mother gives him some warm water and asks him to sleep. Rahul feels better after getting some rest.



Questions

1. How does Rahul feel?


2. What does he have?


3. Where does he decide to rest?


4. What does his mother give him?


5. How does he feel after resting?
