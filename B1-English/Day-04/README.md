2. Grammar: Past Perfect Tense

Structure

Subject + had + V3

Use

Past Perfect is used for an action that happened before another action in the past.

Examples

I had finished my homework before I went outside.

She had completed the project before the deadline.

They had left the station before the train arrived.


Negative

I had not finished my work.

She had not called me before the meeting.


Questions

Had you completed the task before lunch?

Had they arrived before the event started?
