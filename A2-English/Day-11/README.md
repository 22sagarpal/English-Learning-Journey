2. Grammar — Prepositions of Place & Movement

📍 Place

in = ke andar

I am in the room.


on = ke upar

The book is on the table.


under = ke neeche

The bag is under the chair.


behind = ke peeche

The car is behind the house.


between = do cheezon ke beech

The bank is between the shop and the hotel.


🚶 Movement

to = ki taraf

I am going to college.


from = se

I am coming from Delhi.


into = andar ki taraf

She walked into the room.

4. Reading Practice 📖

Read this:

> Rahul goes to college by bus every day. His college is about five kilometers from his home. He usually leaves home at 8 a.m. The bus stops near his house. He gets off the bus near the college and walks for five minutes to reach the main gate.



Questions

1. How does Rahul go to college?


2. How far is his college?


3. When does he leave home?


4. Where does the bus stop?


5. How long does he walk?
