2. Grammar — Relative Clauses

Relative Clauses का इस्तेमाल किसी person, place या thing के बारे में extra information देने के लिए होता है।

Who → People 👤

> The man who lives next door is a doctor.



> I have a friend who speaks three languages.



Which → Things/Animals 📱🐕

> This is the laptop which I bought yesterday.



> I watched a movie which was very interesting.



That → People या Things

> The book that I bought is useful.



> The teacher that teaches us English is friendly.



Where → Places 📍

> This is the college where I study.



> Delhi is a city where many people work.

3. Combine the Sentences

Join the two sentences using who, which, that, or where.

Example:

I have a friend. He lives in Delhi.

➡️ I have a friend who lives in Delhi.

Now you:

1. I know a teacher. She teaches English.


2. This is the laptop. I bought it last year.


3. This is the college. I study there.


4. I watched a movie. It was very interesting.


5. He is the student. He won the competition.

5. Reading Practice 📖

> My college is a place where I have learned many useful things. I have several friends who always support me. One of my friends is very creative and often helps me with projects that require new ideas. We have a computer laboratory which has many useful resources. There are also teachers who encourage us to improve our technical skills.



Questions

1. What is the college a place where the writer has learned?


2. Who supports the writer?


3. Who helps with projects?


4. What does the computer laboratory have?
