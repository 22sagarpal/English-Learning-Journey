3. Grammar – Inversion

Normal:

> I have never seen such a beautiful place.



Advanced:

> Never have I seen such a beautiful place.



Examples:

Rarely do we encounter such talent.

Little did I know what would happen next.

Not only did he win the competition, but he also set a record.



---

4. Reading Practice

Read this paragraph:

> Success is rarely the result of talent alone. More often, it emerges from consistent effort, resilience in the face of adversity, and the willingness to learn from failure. Individuals who embrace challenges tend to develop skills that ultimately distinguish them from others.



Questions

1. What is success mainly based on?


2. What helps people develop valuable skills?


3. What does "adversity" mean?
