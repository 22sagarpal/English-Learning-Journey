2. Grammar — Adjectives & Word Order

Adjective kisi person, place ya thing ki quality/description batata hai.

Basic Structure

Adjective + Noun

a red shirt

a big house

a beautiful dress

an expensive phone


❌ a shirt red
✅ a red shirt

Multiple Adjectives

A2 level par simple combinations practice karo:

a beautiful black shirt

a small blue bag

an expensive new phone

a comfortable white T-shirt



---

3. Grammar: Too & Enough

Too = Zaroorat se zyada

This shirt is too tight.

The bag is too expensive.

The water is too hot.


Enough = Parayapt / Jitna chahiye

The shirt is comfortable enough.

The room is big enough.

I have enough money.


🧠 Difference:

> This shirt is too small. ❌
This shirt is big enough. ✅
