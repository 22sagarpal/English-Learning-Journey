2. Advanced Connectors

C1 English mein simple "and, but, because" se aage jaana hai.

Instead of "but"

However

Nevertheless

Nonetheless

On the other hand


Instead of "because"

Since

Given that

Due to the fact that

In light of the fact that


Instead of "so"

Therefore

Consequently

As a result

Hence


Example

❌ Technology is useful, but it can be dangerous.

✅ Although technology offers substantial benefits, it can nevertheless create significant risks.


---

3. Grammar: Advanced Conditionals

Mixed Conditional

Past condition + present result:

> If I had studied English earlier, I would be more confident now.



Present condition + past result:

> If I were more disciplined, I would have completed the course by now.



More Examples

If I had started earlier, I would have achieved better results.

If he were more careful, he wouldn't have made that mistake.

Had I known about the opportunity, I would have applied.


"Had I known..." = advanced C1 structure.

4. Speaking Practice 🎤

Speak for 4–5 minutes:

Topic:

"Is Social Media More Beneficial or Harmful to Society?"

Use these words:

controversial

substantial

undermine

nevertheless

consequently


Structure:

Introduction → Advantages → Disadvantages → Your opinion → Conclusion

Try not to translate from Hindi word-by-word.


---

5. Reading Practice 📖

Read this carefully:

> Although social media has made communication faster and more accessible, its influence is not entirely positive. Excessive usage may undermine people's ability to maintain meaningful face-to-face relationships. Nevertheless, social platforms can provide substantial educational and professional opportunities when used responsibly.



Questions

1. What positive change has social media brought?


2. How can excessive usage affect relationships?


3. What opportunities can social media provide?


4. Find a word that means "weaken".


5. Find a connector showing contrast.
