3. Grammar – Nominalisation

Nominalisation means changing verbs/adjectives into nouns to make writing more formal.

Normal

> The company expanded rapidly.



Formal

> The company experienced rapid expansion.



Examples

The government decided to act. → The government's decision led to action.

People improved their skills. → Skill improvement was evident.

The economy grew quickly. → Rapid economic growth occurred.


This structure is very common in academic and business English.

Nations that invest strategically in these areas tend to experience sustainable growth and enhanced living standards.
