2. Grammar — Because & So

Because = Kyunki

Kisi action ka reason batane ke liye.

I am happy because I passed my exam.

I am tired because I studied all day.

She is nervous because she has an interview.


So = Isliye

Kisi situation ka result batane ke liye.

I was tired, so I went to bed.

It was raining, so I stayed at home.

I was hungry, so I ate some food.


🧠 Easy Trick

Because → Reason

> I stayed home because I was sick.



So → Result

> I was sick, so I stayed home.

4. Reading Practice 📖

Read this:

> Riya was nervous because she had an important exam. She studied hard for several days. On the day of the exam, she felt more confident. She completed the exam successfully, so she was very happy. After the exam, she went home and relaxed.



Questions

1. Why was Riya nervous?


2. How long did she study?


3. How did she feel on the exam day?


4. Why was she happy?


5. What did she do after the exam?
