2. Grammar: Present Continuous vs Present Simple

Present Simple

Daily habits aur routines ke liye.

Structure:
Subject + Verb (1st form)

Examples:

I study English every day.

She plays badminton.

We go to college.


Present Continuous

Abhi jo kaam ho raha hai uske liye.

Structure:
Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing

Examples:

I am studying English now.

She is playing badminton.

We are going to college.


Difference

I study English every day. ✅ (Habit)

I am studying English now. ✅ (Right now)
