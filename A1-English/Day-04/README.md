2. Grammar – Singular & Plural

Singular (एक)

book

pen

apple

student


Plural (एक से अधिक)

books

pens

apples

students


Examples

This is a book. → These are books.

That is an apple. → Those are apples.

He is a student. → They are students.



---

3. Grammar – This, That, These, Those

This (यह) – पास और एक

This is a book.

This is my pen.


That (वह) – दूर और एक

That is a car.

That is a tree.


These (ये) – पास और कई

These are books.

These are flowers.


Those (वे) – दूर और कई

Those are cars.

Those are trees.
