2. Grammar: Passive Voice (Present & Past)

Present Passive

Subject + am/is/are + V3

Examples:

English is spoken in many countries.

The project is completed on time.

Emails are sent every day.


Past Passive

Subject + was/were + V3

Examples:

The work was finished yesterday.

The meeting was held on Monday.

The report was submitted last week.


Practice

Change into Passive Voice:

1. They complete the project.


2. She writes a letter.


3. The team finished the task.


4. The teacher explained the lesson.
