2. Grammar – Linking Words 🔗

Linking words ideas ko connect karte hain.

Addition – Extra information

Moreover / Furthermore / In addition

> English is useful. Moreover, it can improve career opportunities.



> The course is affordable. Furthermore, it provides practical experience.




---

Contrast – Opposite idea

However / Nevertheless / Although / Whereas

> The job is difficult. However, it is well paid.



> Although the course is challenging, it is extremely useful.



> I prefer online learning, whereas my brother prefers traditional classes.




---

Cause – Reason

Because / Since / As

> I practice English because I want to communicate confidently.




---

Result

Therefore / Consequently / As a result

> He practiced regularly. Therefore, his English improved.



> The company reduced its expenses. Consequently, its profits increased.




---

Example

For example / For instance

> Technology has changed education. For example, students can now attend online classes.




---

3. Complex Sentences 🧠

Simple

> I study English. I want to improve my communication.



Better

> I study English because I want to improve my communication skills.



B2 Level

> Although improving English requires consistent effort, I believe that regular practice can significantly improve my communication skills.



🔥 Target: Although + idea + , + main idea

5. Reading Practice 📖

Read carefully:

> Technology has transformed education in many ways. Students can now access educational resources from almost anywhere, which makes learning more flexible and convenient. However, online education also has certain disadvantages. For instance, students may become distracted or feel isolated without regular interaction with teachers and classmates. Nevertheless, when used effectively, technology can significantly improve access to education and provide learners with opportunities that were previously unavailable.



Questions

1. How has technology transformed education?


2. What are two disadvantages of online education?


3. Why might students feel isolated?


4. What is the writer's overall opinion about technology in education?

8. Daily Conversation

A: Do you think technology has improved our lives?

B: Yes, I believe technology has improved our lives significantly. For example, communication has become much faster and more convenient.

A: Are there any disadvantages?

B: Certainly. Although technology provides many benefits, excessive use can lead to distraction and dependence.

A: So, should we avoid technology?

B: Not necessarily. In my opinion, we should use it responsibly. If we maintain a healthy balance, we can benefit from technology without becoming overly dependent on it.
