2. Grammar — Advanced Modal Verbs

C1 level par modals sirf can/could/should tak limited nahi hote.

Must have + V3

Past ke baare mein strong deduction:

> He must have forgotten the meeting.



= I'm almost certain he forgot.

Can't have + V3

> She can't have finished the project already.



= It's almost impossible that she finished it.

Might have + V3

> He might have missed the train.



= Maybe he missed it.

Should have + V3

> You should have informed me earlier.



= Tumhe pehle batana chahiye tha.

Needn't have + V3

> You needn't have bought so much food.



= Tumhe itna food kharidne ki zarurat nahi thi, but you did.

3. Nuance — Same Meaning, Different Strength

C1 English mein certainty ka level express karna important hai.

Strong certainty

> He must be tired.



Medium possibility

> He may be tired.



Weak possibility

> He might be tired.



Very unlikely

> He can’t be tired.



Ye difference tumhari English ko much more natural banata hai.

4. Advanced Opinion Expressions 🎤

Instead of:

❌ "This is wrong."

Say:

This argument is difficult to justify.

There are serious flaws in this argument.

I am not entirely convinced by this viewpoint.


Instead of:

❌ "I disagree."

Say:

I would challenge that assumption.

I see the point, but I would argue otherwise.

I am not entirely persuaded by that argument.

5. Speaking Practice

"Is Online Education Better Than Traditional Classroom Education?"

Speak for 5–6 minutes.

Introduction → Advantages → Disadvantages → Counterargument → Your Position → Conclusion

6. Reading Practice 📖

> Online education has undoubtedly expanded access to learning. Students can attend courses regardless of their geographical location, while digital resources can enhance the learning experience. Nevertheless, online education may not be suitable for everyone. Students who require direct supervision or face-to-face interaction might struggle to remain motivated. A balanced approach may therefore be more effective than completely replacing traditional education.



Questions

1. What is one major advantage of online education?


2. Why might some students struggle?


3. What solution does the passage suggest?


4. Find a word meaning "improve".


5. Find a word expressing possibility.
