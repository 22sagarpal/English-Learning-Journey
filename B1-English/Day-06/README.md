2. Grammar — Reported Speech 🗣️

Reported Speech का इस्तेमाल किसी दूसरे व्यक्ति की कही हुई बात को अपने शब्दों में बताने के लिए होता है।

Direct Speech

> Rahul said, "I am tired."



Reported Speech

> Rahul said that he was tired.



कुछ Common Changes

Direct	Reported

am/is → was	are → were
have/has → had	will → would
can → could	may → might
today → that day	tomorrow → the next day
yesterday → the previous day	


Examples

Direct: She said, "I am busy."
Reported: She said that she was busy.

Direct: He said, "I will help you."
Reported: He said that he would help me.

Direct: Ravi said, "I can solve this problem."
Reported: Ravi said that he could solve that problem.
