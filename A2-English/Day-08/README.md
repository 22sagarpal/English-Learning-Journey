2. Grammar: Future with "Going To"

Structure

Subject + am/is/are + going to + Verb (1st Form)

Examples

I am going to study tonight.

She is going to visit her friend.

We are going to play cricket.


Negative

Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + Verb

Examples:

I am not going to watch TV.

He is not going to travel tomorrow.


Questions

Am/Is/Are + Subject + going to + Verb?

Examples:

Are you going to study today?

Is she going to buy a new phone?
