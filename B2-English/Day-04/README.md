2. Grammar – Passive Voice

Present Simple Passive

Subject + am/is/are + V3

English is spoken worldwide.

The project is completed every month.


Past Simple Passive

Subject + was/were + V3

The letter was sent yesterday.

The work was finished on time.


Future Passive

Subject + will be + V3

The meeting will be held tomorrow.

The report will be submitted next week.


Active → Passive Examples

Active: The teacher teaches English.

Passive: English is taught by the teacher.

Active: They completed the project.

Passive: The project was completed by them.


Exercise

Change into Passive Voice:

1. The company hires new employees.


2. She wrote a report.

4. The manager reviews the applications.


5. We completed the assignment.

6. Daily Conversation

A: Do you prefer working alone or in a team?

B: I prefer working in a team because different people bring different ideas and skills. Teamwork also helps complete tasks more efficiently.

A: What is the biggest advantage of teamwork?

B: The biggest advantage is that complex problems can be solved more effectively when people collaborate.
