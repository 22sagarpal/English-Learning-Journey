2. Grammar — Past Continuous

Past Continuous ka use kisi aise kaam ke liye hota hai jo past mein kisi particular time par chal raha tha.

Structure

Subject + was/were + Verb-ing

Was → I / He / She / It

I was studying.

He was working.

She was cooking.


Were → You / We / They

You were studying.

We were working.

They were playing.



---

3. Past Continuous + When

Jab ek kaam chal raha tha aur beech mein doosra kaam hua:

> I was studying when my friend called me.



> She was cooking when the phone rang.



> We were watching TV when the electricity went out.



🧠 Remember

Long action → Past Continuous
Short/interruption action → Simple Past

Example:

> I was sleeping when my mother called me.

5. Reading Practice 📖

Read this:

> Yesterday evening, Rahul was studying in his room. His sister was watching TV in the living room. His mother was cooking dinner. Suddenly, the electricity went out. Rahul stopped studying and went outside with his family.



Questions

1. What was Rahul doing?


2. Where was his sister?


3. What was his mother doing?


4. What happened suddenly?


5. What did Rahul do after the electricity went out?

6. Writing Practice ✍️

Write 8–10 sentences on:

"What I Was Doing Yesterday"

Try to use:

was

were

studying

watching

working

when

suddenly


Example:

> Yesterday evening, I was studying English. My family was watching TV. I was practicing grammar when my friend called me. We talked for a few minutes, and then I continued studying.
