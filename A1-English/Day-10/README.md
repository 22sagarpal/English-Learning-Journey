2. Grammar – Present Continuous Tense

जब कोई काम अभी चल रहा हो, तब Present Continuous Tense का प्रयोग करते हैं।

Formula

Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing

Examples

I am studying.

You are reading.

He is playing.

She is writing.

We are learning English.

They are watching TV.



---

3. Negative Sentences

I am not sleeping.

He is not playing.

They are not watching TV.


Short Forms

I'm not studying.

He isn't playing.

They aren't watching TV.

4. Question Form
