2. Grammar – Imperative Sentences

Imperative sentences में हम किसी को command, instruction या advice देते हैं।

Basic Formula

V1 + Object

Open the door.

Close the window.

Sit down.

Stand up.

Listen to me.

Read the book.


यहाँ subject “you” आमतौर पर लिखा नहीं जाता।

(You) Open the door. → Open the door.


---

3. Negative Imperative

किसी को कुछ न करने के लिए:

Formula

Don't + V1

Don't run.

Don't shout.

Don't worry.

Don't open the door.

Don't touch this.


⚠️

❌ Don't to run.
✅ Don't run.


---

4. Polite Requests 🙏

सिर्फ command की जगह Please लगाने से sentence ज्यादा polite हो जाता है।

Please sit down.

Please open the door.

Please help me.

Please wait here.

Please speak slowly.

Please repeat that.


“Could you...?” भी सीखो

A1 पर basic level में इसे पहचानना useful है:

Could you help me?

Could you repeat that?

Could you speak slowly?



---

5. Useful English Expressions

जब समझ न आए:

Sorry, I don't understand.

Please speak slowly.

Please repeat that.

Could you say that again?


रास्ते में किसी का ध्यान चाहिए:

Excuse me.

Example:

> Excuse me, where is the bus stop?
