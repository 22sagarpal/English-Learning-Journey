2. Grammar – Can / Can't

Can = सकता हूँ / सकते हैं (Ability)

Can't = नहीं सकता / नहीं सकते

Formula

Subject + can + verb

Subject + can't + verb

Examples

I can speak English.

I can play cricket.

She can sing.

He can swim.

I can't drive a car.

They can't fly.

3. Question Form

Can + Subject + Verb?

Can you swim?

Answers

Yes, I can.

No, I can't.

Yes, she can.
