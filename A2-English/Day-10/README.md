2. Grammar: Infinitives (to + Verb)

Infinitive ka use purpose, plan ya intention batane ke liye hota hai.

Structure

Subject + Verb + to + Verb (Base Form)

Examples

I want to learn English.

She likes to read books.

We need to save money.

He plans to start a business.


More Examples

I hope to get a good job.

They decided to travel by train.

She wants to become a doctor.

4. Reading Practice

Questions

2. When does he plan to start a business?


3. What does he save every month?


4. What does he learn?
