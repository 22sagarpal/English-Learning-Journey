2. Grammar: Modal Verbs (Can, Could, Should)

Can

Ability ya permission ke liye.

Examples:

I can speak English.

She can drive a car.

Can you help me?


Could

Past ability ya polite request ke liye.

Examples:

I could swim when I was ten.

Could you open the door, please?


Should

Advice dene ke liye.

Examples:

You should study every day.

We should exercise regularly.
