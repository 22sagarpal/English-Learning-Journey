2. Grammar — Gerunds & Infinitives

आज का main topic है:

Verb + -ing (Gerund)
और
to + Verb (Infinitive)


---

3. Gerund — Verb + ing

कुछ verbs के बाद -ing आता है।

Common verbs:

enjoy, avoid, finish, keep, practice, suggest

Examples:

> I enjoy reading books.



> She avoids using her phone during class.



> I finished doing my homework.



> You should practice speaking English.



⚠️ गलत:

❌ I enjoy to read books.

✅ I enjoy reading books.


---

4. Infinitive — to + V1

कुछ verbs के बाद to + verb आता है।

Common verbs:

want, need, decide, plan, hope, expect, promise

Examples:

> I want to learn English.



> She decided to study harder.



> We plan to start a business.



> I hope to get a good job.



⚠️ गलत:

❌ I want learning English.

✅ I want to learn English.


---

5. Important Difference 🔥

Enjoy → ing

> I enjoy playing cricket.



Want → to + verb

> I want to play cricket.



Avoid → ing

> I avoid wasting time.



Decide → to + verb

> I decided to improve my English.
