2. Grammar – Possessive Adjectives

Possessive	Meaning

My	मेरा / मेरी
Your	तुम्हारा / आपकी
His	उसका (लड़का)
Her	उसकी (लड़की)
Its	उसका (वस्तु/जानवर)
Our	हमारा
Their	उनका


Examples

My name is Sagar.

Your book is new.

His brother is a student.

Her sister is a teacher.

Our college is big.

Their house is beautiful.
