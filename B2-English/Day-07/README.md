2. Grammar – Modal Verbs

B2 level par sirf can/could nahi, balki modals ke different meanings samajhna important hai.

🔹 Ability

Can / Could

> I can speak English.



> I could swim when I was ten.



🔹 Possibility

May / Might / Could

> It may rain today.



> He might be busy.



> She could be at home.



🔹 Advice

Should / Ought to

> You should practice English every day.



> You ought to improve your vocabulary.



🔹 Obligation

Must / Have to

> You must submit the assignment today.



> I have to attend college tomorrow.



🔹 No Necessity

Don't have to / Needn't

> You don't have to come tomorrow.



Meaning: Tumhe aana zaroori nahi hai.

🔹 Strong Deduction

Must

> He has been working all day. He must be tired.



Yahan must = strong assumption.

3. Advanced Modal Practice 🧠

Difference samjho:

Must

Strong certainty:

> She must be tired.



Might

Weak possibility:

> She might be tired.



Should

Expectation:

> She should arrive soon.

7. Daily Conversation

A: What skills should students develop before entering the job market?

B: Students should develop both technical and soft skills. Technical knowledge can help them perform their jobs, while communication and teamwork can help them work effectively with others.

A: Do you think technical skills alone are enough?

B: No, I don't think so. A person may have excellent technical knowledge, but they also need to communicate their ideas and solve problems effectively.

A: What are you doing to prepare yourself?

B: I am developing my technical skills and improving my English communication. I also try to practice consistently so that I can become more confident.
