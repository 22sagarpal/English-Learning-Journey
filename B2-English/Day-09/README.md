2. Grammar – Gerunds

Gerund = Verb + ing, jo sentence mein noun ki tarah kaam karta hai.

Examples

> Learning English takes time.



> Reading improves vocabulary.



> I enjoy playing cricket.



> She avoids using her phone while studying.



Common verbs + Gerund

Inke baad generally -ing aata hai:

enjoy

avoid

finish

suggest

consider

keep

mind

practice


Examples

> I enjoy learning English.



> He avoids wasting time.



> She suggested going by train.



❌ I enjoy to learn English.
✅ I enjoy learning English.


---

3. Grammar – Infinitives

Infinitive = to + Verb

Examples

> I want to learn English.



> She decided to study abroad.



> He hopes to get a good job.



Common verbs + Infinitive

want

decide

hope

plan

promise

agree

refuse

learn

need

expect


Examples

> I plan to improve my communication skills.



> They decided to start a business.




---

4. Gerund vs Infinitive 🧠

Compare:

> I enjoy reading books.



> I want to read this book.



Enjoy → Gerund

Want → Infinitive

Some verbs can take both:

> I started learning English.



> I started to learn English.



Dono generally correct hain.

6. Reading Practice 📖

Read carefully:

> Developing a new skill requires patience and consistent effort. Many learners enjoy discovering new information, but they often struggle to maintain their motivation over a long period. One effective approach is to set realistic goals and keep practicing regularly. Instead of trying to become perfect immediately, learners should focus on making gradual progress. This can help them build confidence and avoid giving up when they face difficulties.



Questions

1. What does developing a new skill require?


2. What do many learners struggle to maintain?


3. What is one effective approach mentioned in the paragraph?


4. Why should learners focus on gradual progress?

7. Speaking Practice 🎤

Speak for 5 minutes:

"How I Am Improving Myself"

Talk about:

Skills you are learning

Why you chose them

What you enjoy learning

What you avoid doing

Your daily routine

Your future goals


B2 Formula:

I enjoy + -ing

> I enjoy learning new technologies.



I want + to + verb

> I want to become more confident.



I avoid + -ing

> I avoid wasting time on unnecessary activities.



I plan + to + verb

> I plan to improve my professional skills.

9. Daily Conversation

A: What do you enjoy doing in your free time?

B: I enjoy learning new skills and reading about technology. I also like watching educational videos.

A: What skill do you want to improve the most?

B: I want to improve my English communication skills because I believe they will help me professionally.

A: What do you avoid doing?

B: I try to avoid wasting too much time on activities that don't contribute to my goals.
