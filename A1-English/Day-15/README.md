2. Grammar – Simple Past

जब कोई काम past में हो चुका है, तो Simple Past का इस्तेमाल करते हैं।

Regular Verbs

अधिकतर regular verbs में -ed जोड़ते हैं।

Present	Past

play	played
work	worked
watch	watched
visit	visited
walk	walked
talk	talked
clean	cleaned
help	helped
start	started
finish	finished


Examples

I played cricket yesterday.

I watched a movie last night.

She cleaned her room.

He worked yesterday.

We visited our friend.



---

3. Important Rule ⚠️

Past में did आने पर verb वापस अपने original form में आता है।

❌ Did you played cricket?
✅ Did you play cricket?

❌ I didn't watched TV.
✅ I didn't watch TV.

Formula

Question:
Did + Subject + V1?

Negative:
Subject + did not + V1


---

4. Negative Sentences

I did not play cricket.

He did not watch TV.

She did not study yesterday.

We did not visit the park.


Short form:

did not = didn't

I didn't play.

He didn't watch.

They didn't study.



---

5. Questions

Did you play cricket?

Did you watch TV?

Did she study yesterday?

Yes, I did.
