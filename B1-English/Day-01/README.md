2. Grammar: Present Perfect Tense

Structure

Subject + has/have + V3

Examples

I have completed my homework.

She has finished her project.

We have learned English for one year.


Negative

I have not completed my homework.

She has not finished her project.


Questions

Have you completed your homework?

Has she finished her project?
