2. Grammar: Present Perfect (Basic)

Present Perfect ka use un kaamon ke liye hota hai jo past mein hue hain aur unka asar abhi bhi hai.

Structure

Subject + has/have + Verb (3rd Form)

Examples

I have finished my homework.

She has completed her project.

We have visited Jaipur.

He has learned English.


Negative

I have not finished my work.

She has not called me.


Questions

Have you finished your homework?

Has he reached home?
