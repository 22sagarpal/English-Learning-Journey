2. Grammar – Am, Is, Are

Use of "Am"

I am a student.

I am happy.


Use of "Is"

He is a boy.

She is a girl.

It is a book.


Use of "Are"

You are my friend.

We are students.

They are happy.


Formula

Subject + am/is/are + noun/adjective

Examples:

I am Sagar.

She is a teacher.

We are friends.
